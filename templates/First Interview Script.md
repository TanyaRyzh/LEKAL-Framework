# Idea Clarification Interview Script

This script helps the consultant collect the information required to create an **Idea Context Snapshot**.

The goal of the interview is not to design the product, but to understand the context surrounding the idea.

The conversation typically takes **30–60 minutes**.

---

# Section 1 — Understanding the Idea

Start by allowing the client to explain the idea freely.

Suggested prompt:

> Could you describe the idea in your own words?

Follow-up questions:

- What inspired this idea?
- When did this problem first appear?
- Why is it important to solve it now?

The goal is to capture the **initial idea description**.

---

# Section 2 — Problem Context

Focus on the situation that led to the idea.

Questions:

- What problem are you trying to solve?
- Who experiences this problem?
- How often does it occur?
- What happens if the problem remains unsolved?

The goal is to understand the **business context** of the idea.

---

# Section 3 — Target Users

Identify who will interact with the product.

Questions:

- Who are the main users of the product?
- Are there multiple types of users?
- Who benefits from the system even if they do not use it directly?

Capture:

- primary users
- secondary users
- stakeholders

---

# Section 4 — Expected Outcomes

Clarify what success looks like.

Questions:

- What result do you expect from the product?
- What would change after the product is implemented?
- How would you know the product is successful?

This helps identify the **desired outcome**.

---

# Section 5 — Core Interaction

Identify the central action performed in the system.

Questions:

- What will users actually do in the product?
- What is the main action or workflow?
- What is the most important capability the product should provide?

The goal is to capture the **core interaction hypothesis**.

---

# Section 6 — Current Solutions

Understand how the problem is currently handled.

Questions:

- How is this problem solved today?
- Are there existing tools or systems involved?
- What works well in the current solution?
- What does not work well?

This reveals potential opportunities and limitations.

---

# Section 7 — Constraints

Identify known limitations.

Questions:

- Are there any time constraints?
- Is there a defined budget?
- Are there existing systems the product must integrate with?
- Are there regulatory or compliance considerations?

Constraints often influence product design later.

---

# Section 8 — Assumptions

Surface implicit expectations.

Questions:

- Are there assumptions we are making about users?
- Are we assuming certain data or integrations exist?
- Are we assuming a specific technology stack?

Assumptions should be explicitly documented.

---

# Section 9 — Open Questions

Identify areas that require further clarification.

Questions:

- What information is still missing?
- Are there decisions that need to be made later?
- Is there anything we should investigate before moving forward?

---

# Closing the Interview

Before finishing the session, summarize the key points.

Suggested prompt:

> Let me summarize what I understood so far.

Confirm:

- the problem
- the users
- the expected outcome
- the core idea

This ensures that the captured information is accurate.

---

# Output

After the interview, the consultant creates the **Idea Context Snapshot** based on the collected information.

This document becomes the input for the next stage of the LEKAL Framework.
